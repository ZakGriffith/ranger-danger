A new slow-moving tank

Three alternatives to the turtle, drawn in our Kingdom Rush ink & paint direction. Compare their heavy walks and melee attacks, then scrub through any pose.

Heavy walk
A

Rotback Boar

Broad shoulders, short tusks, and overlapping fungal plates. A low animal silhouette with a heavy, deliberate walk.

Attack: Plants its hooves, lifts its head, then drives a tusk sweep forward.

Tank identity: Thick hide and fungal armor. Keeps its slow approach.

B

Rootbound Hulk

A broken, living stump with a face in the bark, branching fists, and roots that drag across the marsh.

Attack: Raises a tangled root arm and brings it down in a heavy smash.

Tank identity: Layers of fibrous bark, with purple infection growing through the cracks.

C

Fungal Brute

A squat, fleshy fungus creature with a broad plum mantle, moss-green body, and oversized knotted fists.

Attack: Braces its body, winds one arm back, and throws a heavy punch.

Tank identity: Dense fungal flesh and broad protective growths.
